Administrator access

Add or update administrators. Each administrator signs in using a single-use email OTP. At least one active administrator must remain.

Employee logins

Sales employees can only create/view invoices and track receivables. All other ERP modules remain restricted. Other staff roles are controlled by their assigned permissions.

Removing a login revokes its sessions and unused codes and removes the account from the authorized user list. Historical invoices and audit logs are retained.
